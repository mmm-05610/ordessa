# C09 external prototype policies

These JSON materials exercise the existing synthetic C09 prototype workflow. They are not `method.yaml` packages and are not interpreted by the new methods loader/compiler. Their method revision and registry binding were explicitly advanced for the current source closure; old stored materials keep their original identities and remain fail-closed on drift. Callers explicitly provide their bodies to the prototype importer; engineering wheels contain neither policy files nor a named bundled-policy lookup.

The prototype executor remains outside the methods mechanism. Its replacement by the application bridge is a separate application migration; these controlled fixtures do not establish that integration.
